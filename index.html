The goal

Small games that respect your time.

Five minutes, one clever idea, something nice to look at. It should feel like a short, calm trip: you think, it clicks, you smile, and you carry on with your day.

  • One idea per puzzleChess pieces, a new rule, a small problem to crack.
  • No accountOpen it and play. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • A place to beEvery chapter is somewhere you might want to visit.

The story

One-man band, three obsessions.

Ontelivo is a one-man band: me, with a weakness for chess, art and travel. Chess Abstract is what happens when all three share one board.

I like the chessboard as a stage rather than a battlefield. Nobody is trying to beat you here. You solve small chess puzzles, each with one rule to crack, and every chapter drops you somewhere worth a postcard: a snowy village, a sunset street, a canal at night. The reward for playing is the trip itself.

No big studio, no pressure, no account: just a small game made carefully. Write to me any time at support@ontelivo.com.

Before release

Play it before everyone else.

I am looking for Android players to try the game on their own phones and tell me what feels wrong. You only need a Google account.

Write to support@ontelivo.com.

  1. Email me from the Google account you use on your phone.
  2. Open the invitation link I send back and install the game from Google Play.
  3. Play when you like and tell me what breaks or confuses you.
