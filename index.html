What the game is about

Chess pieces. New rules. One good idea per level.

You will not be asked to mate in five. Instead you slip a piece past the guards, pass energy from piece to piece, type a vault code with a knight, or find the one square nobody watches. You learn a rule in a move, and then it gets clever.

Each chapter takes you somewhere new and ends with a timed finale that mixes what you learned on the way.
