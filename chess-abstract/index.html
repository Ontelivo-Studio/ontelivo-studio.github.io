How it plays

Same moves, new games.

Every move is a riddle. Each level is a short logic puzzle you solve at your own pace, and each chapter teaches a new game step by step.

  • New questionsFind the one square no piece attacks, slip past guarded squares to reach the flag, clear the board when every move must be a capture, or place rooks in a sudoku-style puzzle.
  • A puzzle every dayCome back for a fresh chess puzzle and keep your streak going. An endless mode lets you replay the games you have unlocked.
  • New to chess?The game shows how every piece moves and how each game works, and a hint is there when you are stuck.

Questions

Chess Abstract FAQ

What is Chess Abstract?

Chess Abstract is a chess puzzle game for Android made by Ontelivo, a one-person indie studio. The pieces move as in chess, but each level is a short logic puzzle with its own goal, and each chapter is set in a different place in the world.

Is Chess Abstract free?

Yes, it is free to download from Google Play. The game can show rewarded ads: short ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, and your progress is saved on your phone.

Do I need to know chess?

It helps to know how the pieces move, but the game can show you how every piece moves and how each game works.

Where can I play it?

On Android, through Google Play.
