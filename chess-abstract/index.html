Chess Abstract

A puzzle game made of short mini-games on a chessboard. Every chapter is a place in the world, and every level is one small rule to crack.

Free to download · No account · Plays offline

The journey

Every chapter is a destination.

There is no story and no villain. The reward for playing is the trip itself. Chapters open one after another, and more places are on the way.

How it plays

Familiar pieces, unfamiliar goals.

Each kind of puzzle has one rule you can say in a sentence. A new kind always starts with a short rule card, so you are never guessing what the game wants. Here is a taste.

Maze

Bring your piece to the diamond without stepping on a square the guards attack.

Chain

Pass energy from the pinned piece, attack by attack, until it reaches a battery.

Vault

Type the code by landing your knight on the digits in the right order.

Hide

Find the one empty square that no guard can see.

Capture

Every move is a capture. Finish with a single piece left on the board.

Walk

Take your king over every free square exactly once. Squares you leave disappear.

Rush

The finale of a chapter: the puzzles you met on the way, back to back, each against the clock.
