Your progress

Do I need an account?

No. The game has no accounts and works without an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the progress is deleted and we cannot bring it back, because we never receive a copy.

Can I move to a new phone?

There is no cloud save at the moment. Please write to us before you switch phones and we will tell you what is possible.

Coins and purchases

I paid but did not get what I bought.

Restart the game with an internet connection first. If it is still missing, write to us and include the order number from your Google Play receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds are too. Open your order history in Google Play and choose to request a refund. Our terms of use explain the rest.

Privacy and ads

What data does the game collect?

Anonymous usage statistics so we can fix and improve the game, and, once ads are switched on, what the ad network needs to show them. The full list is in the privacy policy.

Can I ask you to delete my data?

Yes. Write to us, and see the privacy policy for what we can and cannot find, since the game has no accounts.

Settings

How do I change the language?

Open the game's preferences and choose Language. By default the game follows your phone.

How do I turn off reminders?

The game only sends reminders if you allowed them. You can turn them off in your phone's settings, under Apps, Chess Abstract, Notifications.
