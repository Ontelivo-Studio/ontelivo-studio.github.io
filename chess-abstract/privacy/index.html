Privacy policy

Plain version: the game has no accounts, keeps your progress on your phone, and collects anonymous usage statistics. This page explains the details.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

The controller of your data for Chess Abstract and this website is Goran Košutić, trading as Ontelivo, Serbia ("Ontelivo", "we"). You can reach us at support@ontelivo.com.

Stored on your phone

Chess Abstract keeps your progress, coins, settings and chosen language on your device. We never receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name or email address.

What we collect

The game uses Google's Firebase services. They send us the following, linked to a random identifier that the game generates on your phone (an app instance ID). It does not identify you by name.

WhatExamplesWhy
Game activityLevels started, finished or abandoned, help used, chapters completed, which screens are opened, answers to in-game questions such as whether you are new to chessTo find broken or unfair levels and to improve the game
Device and app informationPhone model, Android version, game version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level), first-open date, crash and performance dataTo fix bugs on specific devices and to understand where the game is played
Settings fetched by the gameFirebase Remote Config sends us your app instance ID when it downloads the latest in-game settings, such as shop offersTo change offers and balance without a new release
RemindersIf you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.To tell you when your pawns are full or a daily gift is ready

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. If you share your daily result using the Share button, the text goes through the sharing app you pick, not through us.

Ads

The game is free and is planned to show ads from Google AdMob. Ads are not shown yet. Once they are, Google and its partners may use your phone's advertising ID and other device data to choose and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the game will first show Google's consent message, and you can change your answer later in the game's settings. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings under Privacy, Ads. We will update this page before ads are switched on if anything changes.

Purchases

If you buy coins or other items, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can give you the item.

Why we use your data

  • Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)): for analytics and for personalised ads, where the law requires consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)): to keep the game working, secure and fair, and to understand in general how it is used, where consent is not required.
  • Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): to deliver purchases you make.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): to keep records the law requires, for example for tax.

Who receives your data

We do not sell your data. It is processed for us by Google (Firebase, Google Analytics, Google Play and, once ads begin, Google AdMob) and, for ads, by ad partners that Google works with. Google may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. When it does, it relies on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to its services: policies.google.com/privacy.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept only for the retention period we set in Google Analytics, currently two months for individual events, after which it is deleted automatically. Aggregated statistics that cannot be tied to a device may be kept longer. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer you and for a reasonable time after, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game. This also removes your local progress.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you may ask to see, correct, delete or export data about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot tell which records are yours unless you give us your app instance ID, which we can explain how to find. Write to support@ontelivo.com.
  • Complaints: you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Chess Abstract is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose operator may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses (see GitHub's privacy statement). If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for changes that matter, tell you in the game. Questions: support@ontelivo.com.
